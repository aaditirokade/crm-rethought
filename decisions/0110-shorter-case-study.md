# 0110 · A case study you can scan in minutes

**Date:** 2026-11-16

**Decision:** every paragraph, card and caption on the case study page is cut to a line or two. Recruiters give a portfolio a few minutes, and the long version lost them before the prototype. The sections stay; only the words go.

**Checks:** typecheck, lint on the changed files and the tests pass.
