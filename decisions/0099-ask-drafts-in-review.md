# 0099 · A draft chosen in Ask really waits in Review

**Date:** 2026-11-12

**Decision:** choosing "Draft it" (or "Draft invite") on one of Ask's suggested actions now adds a real draft to Review, instead of only changing the row's label. The draft is grouped as "Drafted from Ask" under its account, comes first in Review, carries the sources of the answer it came from, and is approved, edited or dismissed like any other line; it also counts in Waiting for you and the Review badge. The row shows "Drafted" with a "Review it" button that opens Review (on the phone, the Approve screen), and Undo takes the draft back out.

**Why:** the author asked where a person could review the draft. The label said it was waiting in Review, but nothing was there.

**Checks:** previewed on the local server: Draft it on the Fernhill answer, then Review it, which opens Review with the note to Priya Nair at the top, with three sources and Approve, Edit and Dismiss. Type check and lint clean.
