# 0032 · The phone recording without a device edge

**Date:** 2026-11-07

**Decision:** on the case study home, the phone recording in the walkthrough loses its dark device border. It sits as a bare screen with rounded corners and the same soft shadow as the desktop recordings, in both themes.

**Why:** the near black edge was the heaviest thing on the page and read as a device mockup. A bare screen matches the desktop recordings and keeps attention on the app.

**Checks:** axe clean in light and dark at 375px and 1440px; no sideways scrolling.
