# 0026 · A chapter timeline beside the case study

**Date:** 2026-11-06

**Decision:** on wide screens (1280px and up) the case study home gets a thin timeline on the left with one dot per chapter: the problem, research, Ask, how it works, design system, built in the open, try it. The dot for the chapter on screen fills in as the page scrolls, and each label jumps to its chapter. Narrower screens read straight down without it.

**Also:** the walkthrough stepper loses its step numbers; both steppers now use a hairline rule and small dots, in ink rather than violet, because violet belongs to the AI.

**Why:** the page is long, and a reader should always know where they are and what is left. Small dots and a hairline keep the timeline quiet next to the content.

**Checks:** axe clean in light and dark at 375px and 1440px; no sideways scrolling.
