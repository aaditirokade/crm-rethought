# 0053 · A tighter case study page

**Date:** 2026-11-07

**Decision:** the visuals on the Groundwork case study get smaller, and text moves up beside them, so sections stop floating in empty space.

- **The problem:** the reel is at most 35rem wide.
  - The number is smaller.
  - A seller's week is a slim row of blocks, 24rem wide.
  - The stale list shows 5 accounts instead of 8 (4 of 5 stale, still about the 76% it stands for).
  - Each beat sits centered in a shorter panel.
- **Research:** the coverage chart is at most 32rem wide, with slimmer bars and tighter rows, starting from the page's left edge.
- **Ask:** the text and the four tiles are top aligned. The tiles are at most 32rem wide together, with smaller padding, titles and body text, and each picture shows in full.
- **Design system:** the swatches are smaller tiles, at most 26rem wide. The visual takes 5 columns, so the text sits closer to it.
- **Keep going between meetings:** the text uses the same narrow column as the other steps, and the phone sits right beside it instead of at the far right.

**Why:** at desktop widths, these visuals were larger than what they show, and the gaps around them read as empty.

**Checks:** axe clean in light and dark at 375px and 1440px; no sideways scrolling; no picture in the Ask tiles is cut off; the problem panel keeps one height across its three beats.
