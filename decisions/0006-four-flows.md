# 0006 · The four flows to build in depth

**Date:** 2026-11-06

**Decision:** build My day, Meeting brief, Log a call and Deals at risk in depth. Source links, confidence and one move record fixes run through all four. Imports and handoffs stay light.

**Options considered:** the nine workflows in the plan · a single hero flow · four flows that share one trust pattern.

**Why:**
- They answer the pains the most interviews raised (P1 to P5 in [synthesis.md](../research/synthesis.md)), and they follow a real day: start the day, prep, talk, log, watch the pipeline.
- Four flows show one trust pattern (sources, confidence, approve in place) used consistently, which is the design idea of the project.
- It is enough to show breadth without building a whole CRM.

**Revisit if:** the design phase shows two flows can merge (the brief and the log may share most of their parts).
