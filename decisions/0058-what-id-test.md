# 0058 · What I'd test, and how I'd measure it

**Date:** 2026-11-10

**Decision:** the case study ends with a "Next" chapter before Try it: the three things I'd test first with account teams, each with the number I'd watch after launch.

- **Do people approve drafts as they are?** Drafts approved without edits, and the edits people make when they don't.
- **Is the record more complete?** Meetings with a logged note within a day, against the CRM people use today.
- **Do people trust Ask?** Answers opened to their source, and questions asked again in other words.

**Why:** every case study on the portfolio now follows the same spine (problem, decisions, the work, the outcome). A concept has no outcome to claim, so it says what it would measure instead of inventing one.
