# 0030 · Try it lines up with the chapter split

**Date:** 2026-11-07

**Decision:** on screens 1024px and wider, the closing "Try it." section is centered on the gap between the chapters' two columns (the 5 and 7 column split) instead of on the page. Below that width the chapters stack, so it stays centered.

**Why:** every chapter above splits the page at the same place. Centering the ending on that line ties it to the layout instead of floating it to the right of the chapters' axis.

**Checks:** the heading, text and buttons share their center with the gap at 1440px; axe clean in light and dark at 375px and 1440px; no sideways scrolling.
