# 0129 · The people in a meeting sit level with the lines that name them

**Date:** 2026-11-19

**Decision:** on a brief, "In this meeting" sits directly beside the first section ("What changed since..."), and each person sits at the height of the line that names them, with a dashed rose pointer reaching across to it (Nora Castell beside "Nora Castell is taking over", Sam Reyes beside "Sam Reyes asked for pricing"). People no line names follow below; people never overlap. The promises and the next section now sit side by side in the row below; pins, how to read and the log link follow on the right of that row. On narrow screens the people stack as a plain list.

**Why:** the people and the changes are about each other; reading across a row answers "who is this about" without hunting.

**Checks:** the positions are measured from the page, so they follow any change in line length or width; screen readers hear each person with the line that mentions them. Typecheck, lint and tests pass.

**Revisit if:** a brief names many people in one line, crowding the column.
