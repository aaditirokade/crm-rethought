# 0119 · Glass leaves and rose leads

**Date:** 2026-11-18

**Decision:** the app gets one material: soft periwinkle blocks that warm to blush (night periwinkle to plum in the dark), with see-through glass leaves growing from a corner. Today's greeting sits on the block with Ask lifting over its edge; every other page carries a quiet band of the leaves behind its title; Waiting for you, the phone's greeting and the bars where you commit (Log, Quick log, Review, Imports) get the leaves too. The palette moves from violet and aqua to periwinkle and rose: rose is the AI and the actions it asks for (primary buttons, Ask, the dock's current step, the phone's current tab), periwinkle is data. The AI's indigo blocks (since 0066) become the same night as the leaves, with rose actions inside.

**Round two (same day):** the leaves stop living only at the top right. Small sprigs of them sit in the corner of Ask and of Lists. (They were on the small cards too, the drafted lines and the AI cards, but looked fussy at that size and came off the same day.) On Today a second, faint layer of shorter leaves wanders toward the middle, rooted behind Ask's top edge.

**Options:** twelve rounds, kept in research/color-exploration: dusk palettes with circles; glass cubes, tetrahedra and prisms on dark blocks; many faced polyhedra; rounded glass cubes; six light patterns (pearl cubes, rose quartz, crystal shards, prism facets, holographic folds, ink marble); grass in two shapes; the broad leaves in six palettes; a blend of periwinkle and rose quartz with either color leading.

**Why:** the art carries the weight, so the colors stay light. On a page that is mostly periwinkle and lavender, rose is the one warm color, so the actions stand out; a periwinkle button blended in. Leaves in more than one place make the whole app read as one thing.

**Checks:** every text token passes AA on bg, surface, surface-2 and draft in both themes (accent 4.7:1 or more by day, 7.1:1 at night; white on the rose 5.6:1). Charts in periwinkle are 3.4:1 or more against the page. The leaves are decorative and hidden from assistive tech, drawn from a seed so the server and the browser match. Typecheck, lint and tests pass.

**Revisit if:** the leaves pull attention from the work in testing, or the rose reads as an alert.
