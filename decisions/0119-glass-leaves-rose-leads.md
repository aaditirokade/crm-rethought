# 0119 · Glass leaves and rose leads

**Date:** 2026-11-18

**Decision:** the app gets one material: soft periwinkle blocks that warm to blush (night periwinkle to plum in the dark), with see-through glass leaves growing from a corner. Today's greeting sits on the block with Ask lifting over its edge; every other page's title sits on a shorter leaves block (it began as a faint band, which looked empty); Waiting for you, the phone's greeting and the bars where you commit (Log, Quick log, Review, Imports) get the leaves too. The palette moves from violet and aqua to periwinkle and rose: rose is the AI and the actions it asks for (primary buttons, Ask, the dock's current step, the phone's current tab), periwinkle is data. The AI's indigo blocks (since 0066) become the same night as the leaves, with rose actions inside.

**Round two (same day):** the leaves stop living only at the top right. (Small sprigs in the corners of the highlights, from cards to Ask and Lists, were tried and taken off: at that size they read as clutter.) On Today a second, faint layer of shorter leaves wanders toward the middle, rooted behind Ask's top edge.

**Ask on Today (same day):** Ask was lavender on the lavender block and merged into it. It is now near white with a soft shadow (a deeper night in the dark), with a thin rose edge fading out from its top left corner, and pale lavender suggestion pills. Text on it is 8.6:1 or more, the pills' text 10.0:1, the field line 4.5:1. Chosen over the edge alone and the white alone.

**Account pulse (next day):** each account tile on Today is a pane of frosted glass, near white with a periwinkle tint, the cooler of the two colors and not both (night periwinkle in the dark), with a bright top edge and a mirror streak that slides across under the pointer. Text keeps its contrast on the near white and the night.

**Health in charts (next day):** the portfolio map on Accounts draws its bubbles in solid, bright health colors (mint, amber, coral; a step deeper at night) with a ring of the page around each, instead of pale tints of the text colors. The set passes the colorblind separation check in both themes; the health words and their shapes stay beside every bubble, so color is never the only cue. The renewing soon zone is a rose wash.

**Accounts and hover (next day):** the map and the table sit on frosted periwinkle glass panes, Lists on the night block with leaves, and every hover wash is periwinkle instead of gray.

**Options:** twelve rounds, kept in research/color-exploration: dusk palettes with circles; glass cubes, tetrahedra and prisms on dark blocks; many faced polyhedra; rounded glass cubes; six light patterns (pearl cubes, rose quartz, crystal shards, prism facets, holographic folds, ink marble); grass in two shapes; the broad leaves in six palettes; a blend of periwinkle and rose quartz with either color leading.

**Why:** the art carries the weight, so the colors stay light. On a page that is mostly periwinkle and lavender, rose is the one warm color, so the actions stand out; a periwinkle button blended in. Leaves in more than one place make the whole app read as one thing.

**Checks:** every text token passes AA on bg, surface, surface-2 and draft in both themes (accent 4.7:1 or more by day, 7.1:1 at night; white on the rose 5.6:1). Charts in periwinkle are 3.4:1 or more against the page. The leaves are decorative and hidden from assistive tech, drawn from a seed so the server and the browser match. Typecheck, lint and tests pass.

**Revisit if:** the leaves pull attention from the work in testing, or the rose reads as an alert.
