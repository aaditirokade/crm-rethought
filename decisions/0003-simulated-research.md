# 0003 · Research: simulated interviews, labeled

**Date:** 2026-11-05

**Decision:** no live interviews in this round. Five simulated interviewees are built from real, linked public sources, and every simulated artifact is labeled SIMULATED.

**Options considered:** real interviews · simulated, labeled · a mix.

**Why:** time. A real round needs recruiting and scheduling that this project cannot wait for. Grounding each persona's pains in a public source keeps them honest; the label keeps readers from taking them as real people.

**Rules:**
- Every pain a persona has points to a source in `research/desk-research.md`
- Every quote from a persona is marked SIMULATED
- Real interviews and usability sessions are listed as the next step, not skipped silently

**Revisit if:** time opens up for 3 to 5 real conversations.
