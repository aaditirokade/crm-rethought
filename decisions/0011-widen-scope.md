# 0011 · Widen the prototype: opportunities, every kind of interaction, lists in plain words

**Date:** 2026-11-06

**Decision:** with the meeting loop working, add three features to the prototype, each built on the same rule (AI drafts and explains, a person decides, [decision 0008](0008-ai-drafts-people-decide.md)):

1. **Opportunities with ranked outreach.** Who to reach out to this week, ranked by reasons that each carry a source; a person reorders or snoozes; the AI never contacts a client. New opportunities and stage changes the AI spots wait behind the gate. This replaces the planned "Deals at risk" screen.
2. **The interaction log across types.** Calls, video, in person, events, email and tickets on one filterable timeline, with coverage (who on our side last spoke to each person). Interactions without a recording are logged from the person's own notes. A **high stakes** level is never recorded, drafts only from notes, and hides lines the AI is unsure of.
3. **Lists in plain words.** Type the list you want; the AI shows what it understood as chips you can remove, says why each account matches, and saves only when asked.

**Options considered:** stop at the meeting loop · add these three · build every CRM area at once.

**Why:**
- They were raised by the author as core CRM jobs, and they map to pains in the [synthesis](../research/synthesis.md): quiet deals noticed late (P5), context spread across tools (P4), repeat report requests (P7).
- Each one shows the trust layer in a new situation: ranking with reasons, sensitivity that changes the gate, and a filter you can read before you trust it.

**Revisit if:** the extra screens make the case study harder to follow; the meeting loop stays the lead story.
