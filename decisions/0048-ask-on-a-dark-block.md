# 0048 · Ask on its own dark block

**Date:** 2026-11-07

**Decision:** "Ask about any account" on Today sits on a deep indigo block, in light and dark, so it's the first thing the eye lands on. The phone app's Ask entry on its home screen gets the same block.

- **Tokens, not one off colors:** the block redefines the color tokens inside it, so the input, buttons, source chips, steps and numbers all follow without their own rules.
- **Both themes:** in light, the block is the one dark area on a cream page. In dark, its indigo hue sets it apart from the charcoal around it.
- **Contrast:** text 14.6:1, secondary text 9.4:1, accent 9.7:1, control borders 5.0:1 on the block.
- **Board focus:** while Ask answers, the items it isn't talking about now go gray instead of fading to 45%. Fading dropped their text below AA.

**Why:** Ask is the heart of the product, but it sat in a white card like every other card. The page had no color blocking, so nothing led.

**Checks:** axe clean in light and dark at 375px and 1440px, with an answer open; no sideways scrolling.
