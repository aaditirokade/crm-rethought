# 0134 · The phone app in the new look, and who Daniel is

**Date:** 2026-11-19

**Decision:**
- The walkthrough's phone recording sits in a silver phone, the same brushed band as the home page tile, in both themes.
- On the phone app, Approve, Voice note and the brief put their titles on the leaves block, as Next up does. Ask keeps its own card.
- The question in the phone's Ask field is 18px, so a chosen question fits on one line.
- The brief's Suggested agenda sits on the draft wash with the rose edge, because it is the AI's proposal.
- A source popover opened inside the phone stays on the phone's screen and is phone sized.
- Daniel's role shows where he is named: "Daniel Orro · Account Manager, Larkspur" in the desktop top bar (on wide screens) and under "Hi Daniel" on the phone.

**Why:** the phone had been left plainer than the web app. Its popovers could spill past the phone onto the page around it. And nothing said who Daniel is, which a reader needs in order to judge what he is shown.

**Checks:** the subtitles on the leaves block keep to the plain left side, so the leaves never pass behind text. The popover was measured inside the phone frame on the desktop page and inside the screen on a 375px phone. Typecheck and lint pass. The clips were recorded again so they show the change.

**Revisit if:** the phone app gains new screens.
