# 0029 · The colors as tiles

**Date:** 2026-11-07

**Decision:** in the design system chapter of the case study home, the six colors (Page, Sand, Ink, AI, AI wash, Charts) sit in sand tiles, three across on wider screens and two across on phones. Each tile has a small chip, half light and half dark, with a thin border so the cream and sand halves still read on the tile, then the name and both hex values. The tiles lift a little on hover, like the Ask tiles, and stay still for reduced motion.

**Why:** the large swatches took up the whole visual and read as decoration. Smaller chips in tiles are easier to scan, match the Ask tiles, and leave the names and values as the main content.

**Checks:** axe clean in light and dark at 375px and 1440px; no sideways scrolling.
