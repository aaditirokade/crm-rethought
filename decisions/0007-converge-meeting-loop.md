# 0007 · Converge: build the meeting loop first

**Date:** 2026-11-06

**Decision:** build the Meeting brief and Log a call first, as one connected concept: the brief prepares the call, the log records it, and the log feeds the next brief. My day becomes a light entry point that lists today's meetings. Deals at risk waits until the loop exists. The trust layer (sources, confidence, approve in place, one move fixes) runs through all of it.

**Options considered:** each of the four flows on its own · the meeting loop (brief + log) · all four at equal depth.

**Why:**
- It answers the most pains: P1 and P2, which all five interviews raised, plus P3, P4 and P6 ([synthesis](../research/synthesis.md)).
- It is used most: before and after every meeting, many times a day.
- Its win is measurable: prep from 15 to 40 minutes to under 2; logging from that night to under 1 minute.
- It is the most different from today's CRMs: "what changed since last time", a source on every line, and a log that improves the next brief.
- The other flows depend on it: Deals at risk needs good logs; My day needs the loop's data.

**Risk:** HubSpot is testing a research agent that briefs reps before calls, and Attio logs calls as you speak ([desk research](../research/desk-research.md)). The difference here is the connection between the two and a source on every line.

**Revisit if:** design shows the brief and the log cannot share one pattern, or Deals at risk turns out to be the stronger interview demo.

**Update, same day:** the [competitive analysis](../research/competitive-analysis.md) found Sybill and Sentra already open briefs with what changed. The decision stands; the difference is the trust layer around the loop (a source on every line, approval in place, only approved logs feeding the next brief), not "what changed" alone.
