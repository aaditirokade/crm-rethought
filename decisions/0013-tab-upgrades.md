# 0013 · Pictures instead of lists on every tab

**Date:** 2026-11-06

**Decision:** each tab gets one visual or interaction that answers its main question at a glance, without bending the trust rule:

- **Accounts · portfolio map:** days to renewal against the 8 week usage trend, bubble size by stores, health as icon and word. "Renewing soon and falling" is a shaded corner. The table below keeps the same data as text.
- **Account · who talks to whom:** our people and theirs, lines by number of touches, dashed when quiet for 3 weeks or when the person left; names not yet in the record are marked. Plus an 8 week strip that puts every interaction on the usage line with the moments that explain it (Fernhill: the reorg, Lena leaving).
- **Opportunities · pipeline with ghost moves:** stages as columns. A person can drag a card or use its menu; their own move needs no approval and is stamped. When the AI suggests a move, a dashed ghost card waits in the target column with its reason and source; Approve moves the real card.
- **Review · triage:** one line at a time with keys (A approve, E edit, D dismiss, arrows), and a ring that counts down to zero.

**Why:** the author asked for the other tabs to be as interesting as Today, and each picture shows something a list hides: a risky corner, a lost relationship, the gap between where a deal is and where the AI thinks it is.

**Accessibility:** every chart has a text name and a text version nearby (the accounts table, the interactions list); dragging has a menu alternative; triage keys only work while the triage card has focus; motion respects reduced motion.
