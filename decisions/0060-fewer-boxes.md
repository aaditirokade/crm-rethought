# 0060 · Fewer boxes

**Date:** 2026-11-10

**Decision:** the app stops nesting boxes. The sand and periwinkle blocks from decision 0050 go, and so do the pipeline's column trays, the triage frame and the small boxes inside sections (Ask's figures, the suggested next move, saved record fixes, the people only flag, the email draft, and most of the phone app's cards).

- **Sections** sit on the page, divided by space and a rule, as in decision 0019.
- **A side rail** (Today, a brief, an account) is set off by one vertical rule on wide screens.
- **Places the AI works for you** other than Ask (Lists) carry one violet rule down the left, the same rule a draft row has.
- **Small callouts** inside a section become a short rule on the left instead of a box.
- **Ask stays the one block,** since it's where the AI speaks, with nothing boxed inside it. **Drafts keep their violet tint,** since the tint means "not on the record yet". Pipeline cards stay cards, because you drag them.

**Why:** the author found the screens boxed up, boxes within boxes, and asked to open them up and cut the number of rectangles.

**Checks:** type check and lint clean; the open layout previewed on every page before it shipped.
