# 0116 · The story in six lines as numbered tiles

**Date:** 2026-11-18

**Decision:** the story in six lines is six numbered tiles, 01 to 06, three across on a wide screen, two on a tablet and one on a phone. Each tile shows its number in the AI violet, the step and its line, on the surface color with a hairline border.

**Why:** the author wants the story as numbers and tiles, so each step reads on its own and the order is plain at a glance.

**What it cost:** the section is taller than the old timeline.

**Checks:** typecheck and lint pass.
