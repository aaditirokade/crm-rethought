# 0001 · Clean room: nothing from past employers

**Date:** 2026-11-05

**Decision:** CRM Rethought is designed from scratch. It uses no screens, data, internal terms, metrics or workflows from any CRM the author worked on at a past employer.

**Options considered:** duplicate an earlier CRM case study and rework it · start a new project in a clean room.

**Why:** the earlier work is under a confidentiality agreement and stays private. A public project has to stand on public knowledge (standard CRM concepts, public reviews, public product pages) and the designer's own skills.

**In practice:**
- A fictional company and sample data, labeled as such
- Only CRM ideas any public CRM has: accounts, contacts, coverage, pipeline, interaction logs, campaigns, tasks
- No prompt in `prompts/` quotes or describes past employer material

**Revisit if:** never; this is a standing rule.
