# 0126 · Flat: no elevation anywhere

**Date:** 2026-11-19

**Decision:** no drop shadows anywhere in Groundwork, the prototype, the phone app or the case study. Surfaces are told apart by their fill and a thin edge; the things that float over the page (source popovers, the map tooltip, the stage menu, the account panel, the dock, the theme button) keep a firmer edge in place of a shadow. What stays: the rose edge on drafts, focus rings, the ring on an account Ask is talking about, and the bright top line on glass.

**Why:** with cards that are not links, lift and shadow promised a click; and flat surfaces suit the glass and color blocks better.

**Checks:** a script walked every page in light and dark and found no element with a drop shadow left. Typecheck passes.

**Revisit if:** a floating panel gets lost against busy content; a firmer edge comes before any shadow.
