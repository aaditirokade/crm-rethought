# 0057 · Colors that pop

**Date:** 2026-11-10

**Decision:** Groundwork drops its warm cream and sand washes for a cool, brighter palette.

- **Page and fills:** cool white (#f7f8fc) and a cool mist (#e5e8f3) in place of cream and sand. Ink moves from warm cocoa to a deep navy ink. At night, a deep navy charcoal in place of warm charcoal.
- **The AI's color:** a brighter ultramarine (#3b3be6) in place of periwinkle indigo. White on it is 7.1:1.
- **Charts:** hot pink (#d23f78 by day, #ff8ab8 at night) in place of soft teal, so data still never reads as the AI.
- **Needs a look:** orange (#b54708) in place of the yellow amber, with a peach wash in place of the yellow one. The warm glow at the top of the page is now a pink glow.
- **Kept:** the design directions page (0007) keeps its original colors, as a record of what was explored.

**Why:** the author found the yellow washes bland and asked for colors that pop.

**Checks:** every text pair recomputed against WCAG AA: body ink 13:1 or more, quiet text 6.1:1 or more, control borders 4.1:1, charts 4.2:1 on the page; dark values 3.5:1 or more for borders and 6.3:1 or more for text. Type check and lint clean.
