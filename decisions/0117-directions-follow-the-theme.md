# 0117 · The three directions follow the theme

**Date:** 2026-11-18

**Decision:** the Ledger, Signal and Margin note cards in "Three directions, one kept" follow the site's theme. Each direction keeps its light palette in the light theme and gets a dark version in the dark theme, drawn from the same hues: a warm near black for Ledger and Margin note, a cool one for Signal, with lifted greens, blues and clays for the accents.

**Why:** the cards held their light palettes as fixed colors, so in the dark theme they sat as three bright panels on a dark page.

**What it cost:** the dark versions are a reading of each direction, not part of the original exploration; the light palettes stay the record.

**Checks:** typecheck and lint pass; checked in the browser in the dark theme.
