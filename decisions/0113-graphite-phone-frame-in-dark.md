# 0113 · A graphite phone frame in the dark theme

**Date:** 2026-11-17

**Decision:** in the dark theme the phone preview's frame is graphite, a dark band lit from the top left with a thin light edge, instead of silver. The light theme keeps the silver frame from decision 0112. Every phone prototype on aaditirokade.com now follows the same pair, so Groundwork matches.

**Why:** a silver frame was the brightest thing on the dark page and pulled the eye away from the screen inside it (the cost noted in 0112).

**What it cost:** two frames to keep in step instead of one.

**Checks:** typecheck, lint and the tests pass.
