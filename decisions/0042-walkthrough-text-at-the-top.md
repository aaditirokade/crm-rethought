# 0042 · Walkthrough text at the top of each row

**Date:** 2026-11-07

**Decision:** in "How it works", each step's text sits at the top of its row, beside the top of its recording, instead of centered on it. **Why:** centered text left a lot of empty space above and below it next to the tall recordings, most of all beside the phone.
