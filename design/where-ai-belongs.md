# Where AI belongs in the meeting loop

**As of:** 2026-11-06 · Closes Phase 2 (Define). Built from the "never without checking" answers in the [SIMULATED interviews](../research/interviews/) and pain P2 in the [synthesis](../research/synthesis.md).

## The rule

**AI may read and draft anything. It may save or send nothing on its own.** See [decision 0008](../decisions/0008-ai-drafts-people-decide.md).

## Step by step

| Step | When | AI does | Person decides | Gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gather | Before | Reads email, tickets, past calls and the record since the last touch | Nothing to do yet | None: read only |
| Brief | Before | Drafts what changed, open promises and people moves, each with a source | Pins, edits or dismisses any line | None: the brief is a draft for one person |
| Fix the record | Before | Spots a fact that looks wrong, like a contact who has left | Approves the fix in one move, right where it shows up | Approve |
| The call | During | Transcribes only if everyone agreed | Runs the conversation | Consent |
| Log | After | Drafts the log, each line linked to its moment in the call | Approves line by line; nothing is saved before that | Approve |
| Follow ups | After | Drafts tasks and the follow up email | Approves the tasks; sends the email themselves | Approve; send is manual |
| Record updates | After | Proposes stage, next step date and new contacts | Approves each change | Approve |
| Next meeting | Later | Uses only approved lines as sources for the next brief | Nothing to do | None |

## Three tiers

1. **AI on its own:** read, gather, rank, draft. Nothing leaves the person's screen.
2. **AI suggests, a person approves:** anything written to the shared record, one line at a time.
3. **People only:** merging records, changing an account's owner (Arjun, interview 04), telling a customer they're at risk (Rosa, interview 05), and sending email to a customer (Daniel, persona 2). The AI can draft; it never acts.

## Why these lines

- All five SIMULATED interviews said AI is useful only if it shows why and a person approves (P2).
- Each "never without checking" answer maps to a tier 3 action above.
- Keeping the brief ungated keeps prep fast: a wrong line in a private draft costs a dismiss, not a bad record.
