# Interview guide

30 minutes, one person, open questions only. Written for real interviews; used here to run the five SIMULATED interviews in [interviews/](interviews/).

## Warm up (3 min)
1. What's your role, and who do you work with most in a normal week?

## A normal day (10 min)
2. Walk me through yesterday, from the first time you opened your CRM to the last.
3. Tell me about the last client meeting you prepared for. What did you look at, and how long did it take?
4. After that meeting, what happened to what you learned?

## Trust and data (7 min)
5. Tell me about the last time the CRM let you down.
6. When you don't trust a record, what do you do?

## AI (7 min)
7. Where do you use AI at work today?
8. What would you never let AI do without you checking first?

## Close (3 min)
9. If you had an assistant for one hour a day, what would they do?
10. What should I have asked that I didn't?

## Rules for the interviewer
- Ask about the last real time, not "usually"
- Don't name a feature before they do
- Follow the emotion: "you sighed there, tell me more"
