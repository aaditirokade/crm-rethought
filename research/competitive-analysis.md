## Evidence, by product

- **Salesforce Agentforce Sales.** Summaries to "walk into every conversation prepared"; Conversation Capture logs calls, emails and events; deal scores show "recent positives and negatives"; pipeline updates can be reviewed in "suggestive mode, or update automatically". A change summary exists for territories, not deals. [AI for sales](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/artificial-intelligence/) · [Meeting prep](https://www.salesforce.com/se/agentforce/use-cases/sales-service-prep/)
- **HubSpot Breeze.** Prep for calls and AI-suggested next steps after calls; the Prospecting Agent asks people to approve CRM updates and follow-ups after meetings. A Company Research Agent (beta) and a Customer Health Agent (account churn risk) appear in a third-party guide. [HubSpot AI](https://www.hubspot.com/products/artificial-intelligence) · [Breeze guide, third party](https://www.syncbricks.com/hubspot-breeze-ai-complete-guide-2026/)
- **Attio.** Account briefs and "know which deals need attention"; calls recorded, transcribed and logged as you speak; records update "instantly", with no review step described. [Attio AI](https://attio.com/ai)
- **Gong.** Deal briefs to prepare for the next meeting; automatic capture of interactions and CRM field updates from deal boards; warnings for low engagement, single threading, stalled deals and pricing concerns; an activity timeline. [Platform](https://www.gong.io/platform/) · [Understanding deals](https://help.gong.io/docs/understanding-deals)
- **Clari.** Per a third-party review: call summaries and automatic CRM field updates; deal health scoring and risk flags; deal movement views. clari.com now redirects to Salesloft, so the vendor page was not read. [Clari review by Docket, a competitor](https://docket.io/resources/research/clari-review)
- **Day.ai.** Preps meetings and captures meetings and emails into the CRM; a deal risk flag fires after a recorded meeting; questions surface supporting snippets; "nothing went out without your approval" refers to outbound actions. [Day.ai](https://day.ai/) · [Review by folk, a competitor](https://www.folk.app/articles/day-ai-review)
- **Sybill.** The pre-meeting brief covers every prior call, emails "between meetings" and "any recent changes" to the deal; "auto-updates every CRM field after every sales conversation"; risk flags such as "stakeholder silence" come when you ask; citations in Q&A. [Sybill](https://www.sybill.ai/) · [Pre-meeting brief](https://www.sybill.ai/blogs/ai-pre-meeting-brief-sales-call-prospect-research) · [Deal inspection](https://www.sybill.ai/intelligence/deal-inspection)
- **Sentra.** A company memory; briefs list "what changed: stage, pricing, stakeholders, timeline"; answers come "with citations"; renewals at risk "and why". No CRM log drafting or approval step described. [Sentra](https://www.sentra.app/) · [On brief tools](https://www.sentra.app/articles/best-ai-meeting-brief-tools) · [Use cases](https://www.sentra.app/use-cases)
- **Lightfield.** An AI-native CRM: meeting prep, recaps, deals at risk with reasons in a sample review; a "For review" queue to approve or dismiss drafted emails; follow a roadmap bet "back to the specific calls and threads". [Lightfield](https://lightfield.com/) · [SiliconANGLE, Sep 2026](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/09/ai-native-crm-startup-lightfield-raises-47m-to-build-an-ai-agent-ready-replacement-for-salesforce/)
- **AskElephant.** "Auto-updates every field in your CRM"; human approvals "if needed"; its prep guide suggests asking "What changed in the CRM since our last customer conversation?" [AskElephant](https://www.askelephant.ai/) · [Meeting prep](https://www.askelephant.ai/blog/ai-meeting-prep-for-revenue-teams)
- **Rox.** A knowledge graph over mail, calls and the CRM, sold on "autopilot"; deal risk via CEO Mode stories; reads Salesforce or HubSpot rather than replacing them. [Rox platform](https://www.rox.com/platform)

[Granola for sales](https://www.granola.ai/use-cases/sales) · [Granola on CRM data entry](https://www.granola.ai/blog/eliminate-crm-data-entry-ai-notetakers-auto-populate-pipeline)

## What we take from it

1. **Briefs and automatic logs are now expected.** A CRM that only adds them would not stand out.
2. **Approval is rare, and usually a setting.** Salesforce offers both suggestive and fully automatic modes; Lightfield's review queue covers emails; AskElephant's approvals are optional; Sybill and AskElephant sell automatic CRM updates as the point. Nobody we read builds approval into every line where the AI writes.
3. **Sources are the clearest gap.** None of the thirteen shows a source on every AI line. Sybill, Sentra, Day.ai and Granola cite sources when you ask a question, not on each line of a brief or a log.
4. **"What changed" exists, in two places.** Sybill and Sentra open their briefs with recent changes. Neither links each change to its source or asks before writing it back, so "what changed" alone is not our difference.
5. **So CRM Rethought competes on trust, not features:** a source on every line, a person approving in place, and a log that feeds the next brief only once someone has approved it ([decision 0007](../decisions/0007-converge-meeting-loop.md)).

## Limits

- Marketing pages, not product trials.
- The Clari row rests on a review by Docket, a competitor; two Day.ai cells rest on a review by folk, also a competitor; HubSpot's briefing agent comes from a third-party guide. Sybill's and Sentra's risk flags answer questions rather than alert on their own.
- Re-check before any interview where these products come up; this market changes monthly.
