# Personas · SIMULATED

> **SIMULATED.** These five people do not exist. Each is built from the public evidence in [desk-research.md](desk-research.md); every pain names the finding it comes from (F1 to F7). Quotes are written in their voice and are not real interview quotes. Real interviews are the next step.

All five work at **Larkspur**, a made up B2B SaaS company (about 600 people) that sells a retail analytics platform to mid market and enterprise retailers.

---

## 1. Maya Lindqvist · Account Executive, mid market

- **Her week:** 8 to 12 calls with prospects, a pipeline review every Monday, demos on Thursdays.
- **Goal:** hit quota without dropping follow ups.
- **Pains:** logs calls at night because there is no time between them (F1, F3); walks into calls unsure what changed since last time (F5, context gap).
- **Would trust AI to:** draft the call log and the follow up email.
- **Would not trust AI to:** change a deal stage or a close date without asking.
- **Quote (SIMULATED):** "I sell all day and do data entry all night."

## 2. Daniel Okoro · Account Manager, renewals and expansion

- **His week:** 40 existing accounts, quarterly business reviews, renewal calls 90 days out.
- **Goal:** no surprise churn; spot expansion early.
- **Pains:** account history is spread across email, notes and tickets (F4); contact records are out of date after reorgs (F2).
- **Would trust AI to:** brief him before a review and flag a contact who has left.
- **Would not trust AI to:** email an executive sponsor without his review.
- **Quote (SIMULATED):** "By the time I find the last three emails, the meeting has started."

## 3. Priscilla Chen · Sales Manager, team of 8

- **Her week:** forecast calls, deal reviews, coaching one to ones.
- **Goal:** an honest forecast and reps who sell instead of type.
- **Pains:** the forecast depends on fields nobody updates (F2); she knows the CRM feels like surveillance to her team (F5).
- **Would trust AI to:** flag deals that went quiet, with the reason.
- **Would not trust AI to:** score her reps.
- **Quote (SIMULATED):** "I don't need more dashboards. I need to know which three deals to worry about."

## 4. Arjun Mehta · Sales Operations Analyst

- **His week:** imports, field cleanup, report requests, tool admin.
- **Goal:** clean data and fewer report tickets.
- **Pains:** spreadsheet imports break on formats; duplicates pile up (F2, F4); leadership wants AI but the data is not ready (F4).
- **Would trust AI to:** map import columns, suggest merges, answer simple report questions.
- **Would not trust AI to:** merge records or change permissions without approval.
- **Quote (SIMULATED):** "Every AI pitch assumes our data is clean. It isn't."

## 5. Rosa Delgado · Customer Success Lead

- **Her week:** onboarding calls, health checks, handoffs from sales.
- **Goal:** a smooth handoff and early warning on unhappy customers.
- **Pains:** sales promises live in someone's head, not the record (F3, F5); health signals sit in other tools (F4).
- **Would trust AI to:** summarize what sales promised and surface health signals.
- **Would not trust AI to:** decide an account is at risk without showing why.
- **Quote (SIMULATED):** "The handoff is a forwarded email and a prayer."

---

## What the five have in common

- Time lost before and after conversations, not during them (F1, F3)
- Low trust in the data, which makes them distrust anything built on it (F2, F4)
- Happy to let AI draft; firm that a person approves anything that leaves the building or changes a record
