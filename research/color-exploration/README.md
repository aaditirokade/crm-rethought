# Groundwork color exploration (Nov 2026)

Saved for a future case study section on the color scheme decisions. Images are in `research/color-exploration/` in the crm-rethought repo, numbered in the order they were made.

## Starting point

Groundwork used a cool white page, an electric violet for the AI (#554bf5) and aqua for charts (#009aa3). The goal of the revamp: a glossier look with color blocking, and background art that feels complex enough for an enterprise product.

## The rounds

1. **01 · Three dusk palettes with circles** (navy and rose, violet dusk, navy and copper). Rejected: not glossy enough, and circles felt too soft for an enterprise concept.
2. **02 · Geometric art, dark blocks** (glass cubes in navy, faceted tetrahedra in plum, intersecting prisms in teal). Glossy buttons, frosted glass Ask panel.
3. **03 · Glass cubes in the teal and magenta scheme.** Liked.
4. **04 · Many faced polyhedra** (a geodesic shape, then smaller ones merging, then one shape repeated). Rejected: the cubes were better.
5. **05 · Rounded glass cubes.** Softer corners, magenta only on the top faces.
6. **06 · Going lighter.** The art carries the weight, so the palette moved to pastels (references: holographic film and alcohol ink marble). Pearl cubes and ink marble.
7. **07 · Six light options** (pearl cubes, rose quartz cubes, crystal shards, prism facets, holographic folds, ink marble). The shards read as sword blades and became crystal clusters, then grass.
8. **08 · Grass A (broad glass leaves) against Grass B (long arching blades from the grass references).** Grass A chosen.
9. **09 · Grass A in all six pastel palettes.**
10. **10 · A blend of periwinkle and rose quartz,** with the block warming from periwinkle behind the text to blush behind the leaves. Two ways: periwinkle leads, rose leads.
11. **11 · Both blends in dark mode.** Night periwinkle warming to plum; leaves dimmed and moved clear of the greeting.
12. **12 · The phone app in the rose leads palette, light and dark.**

## Decision so far

Rose leads. On a page that is mostly periwinkle and lavender, the rose action buttons are the one warm color, so they stand out; a periwinkle button blended in. Rose is the AI and actions (button #b8437e light, #bb4384 dark; text #a8376f light, #f59cc8 dark). Periwinkle is data (#6f80d8 light, #8190ec dark).

Contrast checked: white on the rose button 5.1:1 light, 4.5:1 dark (deepened a step for margin); rose draft text 6.1:1 on white cards, 8.5:1 on dark cards; periwinkle chart marks 3.7:1 and 5.8:1 against cards.
